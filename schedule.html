Course Schedule  ·  14 Weekly Sessions + Finals

Schedule

Three sections, one weekly lecture. Section B meets Mondays; Sections A & C meet Thursdays. Each session is ~3 h (1.5 h concept + 1.5 h supervised lab), except the extended labs (Wk 9–10) and the build sessions (Wk 12–14).
Term: 31 Aug – 11 Dec 2026
Section B: Mondays · A & C: Thursdays
Midterm: Wk 8
Final: Wk 15
👥 Section meeting times

The same weekly lecture, labs, and deadlines are delivered to three sections:

  • Section B — Mondays, Robotics Lab. First session: Mon 31 Aug 2026, then every Monday.
  • Section A — Thursdays, Robotics Lab. First session: Thu 3 Sep 2026, then every Thursday.
  • Section C — Thursdays, Robotics Lab (separate time slot). First session: Thu 3 Sep 2026, then every Thursday.

The Date column below is the Section B (Monday) date for each week. Sections A & C meet on the Thursday of that same week (three days later) with identical content. Exact start times are on the LMS.

📖 Reading key

ROS2 = official ROS 2 Humble tutorials (docs.ros.org)  ·  LIMO = AgileX LIMO User Manual  ·  S = Siegwart, Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots 2e  ·  TBF = Thrun/Burgard/Fox, Probabilistic Robotics  ·  TC = The Construct, Build Your First ROS 2 Based Robot
