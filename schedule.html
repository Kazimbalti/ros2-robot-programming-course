Course Schedule  ·  14 Hands-On Lectures + Projects + Finals

Schedule

Three sections, one weekly lecture. Section B meets Mondays; Sections A & C meet Thursdays. Each session is ~3 h: a short concept part (20–30 min) and the rest hands-on labs. Part A (Wk 5–10) is the ROS 2 Bootcamp from the book on turtlesim; Part B (Wk 11–15) applies it to the AgileX LIMO.
Term: 31 Aug – 11 Dec 2026
Section B: Mondays · A & C: Thursdays
Midterm: Wk 8
Final: Wk 15
👥 Section meeting times

The same weekly lecture, labs, and deadlines are delivered to three sections:

  • Section B — Mondays, Robotics Lab. First session: Mon 31 Aug 2026, then every Monday.
  • Section A — Thursdays, Robotics Lab. First session: Thu 3 Sep 2026, then every Thursday.
  • Section C — Thursdays, Robotics Lab (separate time slot). First session: Thu 3 Sep 2026, then every Thursday.

The Date column below is the Section B (Monday) date for each week. Sections A & C meet on the Thursday of that same week (three days later) with identical content. Exact start times are on the LMS.

⚠️ Note

The course was restructured in Week 5 into the 14-lecture hands-on format above. Weeks 13 and 14 are double sessions (two lectures each): the concept parts and core labs are done in class, and the remaining lab steps are finished as homework. Week 15 combines the last lab (real LIMO) with final-mission demos and the final exam; teams that cannot get robot time may demo the final mission in simulation. Dates and lab scope may shift by up to a week for holidays or hardware availability. Any change is announced in class and reflected here. This page is the source of truth.
