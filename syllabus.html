🎯 Course Description

This is a complete, hands-on robotics and ROS 2 curriculum built specifically around the AgileX LIMO. It merges the conceptual spine of the official ROS 2 documentation (Nodes → Topics → URDF → TF2 → Gazebo → Nav2 → Behaviour Trees) with tested university lab exercises, the official AgileX LIMO hardware manual, and a second, from-scratch robot build. Lectures 1–10 run start-to-finish in Gazebo so no one waits for lab time on a physical robot; Lecture 11 puts LIMO's real electronics in your hands subsystem by subsystem; Lectures 12–14 have you build your own ROS 2 robot from a bare motor and an empty microSD card.

🧩 What this course is built from

1. Conceptual depth — ROS 2 terminology and mental models from docs.ros.org, rebuilt line-by-line around LIMO.

2. Tested lab exercises — real package names, launch files, and CLI commands from a university LIMO + ROS 2 module.

3. The AgileX LIMO manual — Lecture 11's six-subsystem hardware deep-dive is grounded in AgileX's own documentation.

4. A from-scratch build — Lectures 12–14 follow The Construct's Build Your First ROS 2 Based Robot reference design end to end.

📝 Course Policies

Attendance

Lecture attendance is expected; laboratory attendance is mandatory because of shared robots, hardware checkouts, and safety procedures. Notify the instructor before missing a lab to arrange a make-up. Full policy on the Attendance page.

Late Work

Each student has a budget of four late days per term, applied in whole-day units, maximum 2 per assignment. After the budget is exhausted, submissions lose 15% per day for up to three days, then receive zero credit. Exams, the capstone demo, and final presentations cannot use late days.

Collaboration & Academic Integrity

Discussing concepts, error messages, and approaches is encouraged. The code and written answers you submit must be your own. Copying code between individuals or teams, sharing solutions, or submitting generated text as your own analysis are integrity violations and are reported per institutional policy. Cite any external code, tutorial, or dataset you build on.

AI Tool Use

AI assistants are permitted for debugging, boilerplate, and explanation. They are not permitted for generating complete lab reports, full driver nodes submitted as your work, or exam answers. Every submission must include a one-line note stating where and how AI tools were used.

Safety

Lectures 11–14 involve LiPo batteries, spinning wheels, a Class 1 LiDAR, soldering, and 3D printing. Follow the lab safety brief: never charge LiPo cells unattended, power down before rewiring, keep fingers clear of drivetrains, and never stare into the LiDAR aperture. Report any damaged battery or frayed wiring immediately.

Accommodations & Well-being

Students with approved accommodations should share documentation within the first two weeks so arrangements can be made. If anything — illness, workload, or circumstances — is getting in the way, contact the instructor early; timely support is far easier to arrange than retroactive relief.
