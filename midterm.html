🧾 Format

PartMarksStyle

🎯 Sample Questions

Representative of the real paper, not exhaustive
  1. A teammate says "topics and services are basically the same, just pick one." Give one situation where a topic is correct and one where a service is correct, and justify each in one sentence.
  2. In "py_node = my_py_pkg.my_first_node:main", name the executable, the module and the function. Which one do you type after ros2 run?
  3. A subscriber prints nothing. List three commands you would run, in order, to find out why.
  4. Write a Python node that subscribes to /number (example_interfaces/msg/Int64) and logs a warning when the value is above 10.
  5. Write the terminal command that spawns a turtle called bob at (3, 3), and explain what the --- in turtlesim/srv/Spawn separates.

✅ How to Prepare

  • Re-do the labs of Lectures 2–4 in a fresh package without looking at the solutions.
  • Be fluent with the CLI: ros2 node/topic/service/interface, ros2 run, colcon build, --ros-args -r.
  • Practise writing a publisher, a subscriber and a service server from a blank file.
  • Answer every warm-up and exit-ticket question of Lectures 2–4 again, and review each "When things go wrong" table.
  • Bring your student ID, a pen, and your one-page cheat-sheet.
⚠️ Missing the midterm

A make-up is offered only for a documented emergency and must be arranged with the instructor before the exam where at all possible. See the Attendance page.
