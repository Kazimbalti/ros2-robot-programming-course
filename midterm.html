🎯 Sample Questions

Representative of the real paper — not exhaustive
  1. A teammate says "topics and services are basically the same, just pick one." Give two concrete situations from this course — one where a topic is correct and one where a service is — and justify each in one sentence.
  2. Here is a URDF joint. It won't spawn the wheel where expected. Identify the error and write the corrected <origin>.
  3. Sketch the TF tree for LIMO in a Nav2 run. Which transform does AMCL provide, and which does the wheel-odometry source provide?
  4. Write a ROS 2 Python node that subscribes to /scan and prints a warning when any range in the forward 30° arc is below 0.4 m. State your assumptions about the message.
  5. You run ros2 launch limo_gazebosim limo_gazebo_diff.launch.py and Gazebo opens but ros2 topic list shows nothing from the robot. List three things you would check, in order.

✅ How to Prepare

  • Re-run Labs 1–7 from a clean workspace without looking at the solutions.
  • Be fluent with the CLI: ros2 node/topic/service/action/param, ros2 run, ros2 launch, colcon build, tf2_echo, view_frames.
  • Practise writing a publisher and a subscriber from a blank file — no template.
  • Review the "Common Mistakes" box at the end of each of Lectures 1–7.
  • Bring your student ID, a pen, and your one-page cheat-sheet.
⚠️ Missing the midterm

A make-up is offered only for a documented emergency and must be arranged with the instructor before the exam where at all possible. See the Attendance page.
