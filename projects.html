Project 1

⚙️ Motors, Power & Compute

Give Forge muscles, a heart and a brain.

  • Gear motors with encoders, L298N + Arduino Nano wiring
  • ros_arduino_bridge firmware, first motion, calibration
  • Power budget, battery choice, power distribution
  • Ubuntu Server + ROS 2 Humble on the robot's computer
Project 2

🧩 ROS 2 Driver, CAD, URDF & Sensors

A nervous system, a body and senses.

  • Custom messages and differential-drive kinematics
  • The driver node: /cmd_vel in, /odom out
  • Onshape CAD → URDF, robot_state_publisher
  • udev rules, LiDAR and camera bring-up
Project 3

🌍 Simulation, 3D Print & Review

A digital twin, a shell and a final review.

  • Parameterised Xacro and Gazebo plugins
  • The launch hierarchy; driving the twin
  • Designing a 3D-printed cover
  • The capstone build review

🧰 No hardware? Do it design-only

Every project also works without buying parts: write the driver against a simulated serial device, build the URDF from CAD, and drive the digital twin in Gazebo. The review then assesses your design package instead of the physical robot.

🎮 GamePut the build in a sensible order
  1. Spin one motor from the microcontroller and calibrate its encoder
  2. Size the battery with a power budget
  3. Install Ubuntu Server + ROS 2 on the robot's computer
  4. Write the driver node: /cmd_vel → wheels, encoders → /odom
  5. Create the URDF from CAD and publish TF
  6. Bring up the LiDAR and camera
  7. Build the Gazebo digital twin and compare it with the real robot
  8. Design and print the cover; final review
