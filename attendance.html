📌 The Rules

ComponentPolicy
Lecture (first 1.5 h)Expected. Attendance is recorded each week. Repeated unexplained absence is raised with the student and, if it continues, per institutional policy.
Lab (second 1.5 h; full session in Wk 9–14)Mandatory. You must be present to check out a robot or hardware kit, to be signed off on the safety brief, and to have lab work marked.
OverallA student with under 75% attendance across the 14 sessions is not permitted to sit the final exam and is graded as incomplete for that component, subject to the department's condonation process.
Exams & capstone milestonesAttendance is compulsory (Midterm Wk 8, Capstone Part 1 Wk 10, Capstone Part 2 Wk 14, Final Wk 15). Absence needs documented grounds.

📝 If You Have to Miss a Session

  1. Tell the instructor first. Email muhammad.kazim@is.uol.edu.pk before the session whenever possible, with the date and reason.
  2. Labs: a make-up lab slot is arranged only when you have notified in advance and a robot/kit and supervision are available — usually within the same week. Unnotified lab absences are not made up.
  3. Lectures: get the lecture page and a classmate's notes; the pen-annotation build of each lecture page is the same content used in class.
  4. Documented absence (illness, bereavement, university-sanctioned activity): send documentation within one week; the session is then excused and does not count against the 75%.
🩹 Excused vs unexcused

An excused absence (documented, or pre-approved) is removed from the attendance denominator. An unexcused absence counts against the 75% threshold. Either way, the lab work still has to be completed — excused just means no penalty for the miss itself.

⏰ Lateness & Early Departure

  • Arriving more than 15 minutes late or leaving early is recorded as a half-absence.
  • Two half-absences count as one full absence toward the 75%.
  • If you know you'll be late, message ahead — a one-off with notice is not penalised.

🤝 Participation

Participation isn't separately graded, but the lab sign-offs, in-class quizzes (see Assignments), and the capstone defence all assume you were in the room doing the work. Consistent engagement is also what makes the borderline-grade and condonation conversations go your way.

✅ Quick checklist
  • Can't make it? Email before the session.
  • Missing a lab? Ask for a make-up slot in the same message.
  • Have a medical/official note? Send it within one week.
  • Keep your own tally — 4 unexcused absences out of 14 already breaches 75%.
